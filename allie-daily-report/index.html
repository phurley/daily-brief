New-job systems beat vague intentions

Benefits setup

Set retirement contributions deliberately

The 2026 employee elective-deferral limit for most 401(k) plans is $24,500; the combined traditional-and-Roth IRA limit is $7,500. Check the employer match, vesting schedule, payroll percentage and investment choices. The ceilings are not automatic targets; preserving near-term cash matters too.

Cash-flow check

Pay-in-four is a scheduled bill

The Federal Reserve reports 16% of adults used BNPL in 2025; among users, 26% reported paying late at least once and 17% reported an extra late-payment charge. Put every installment beside rent, utilities and cards so the full commitment stays visible.
