Money + practical AI

Two setup checks for a new-job year

Capture the match first

The 2026 employee 401(k) limit is $24,500; the IRA limit is $7,500. For a single filer, the Roth IRA contribution phase-out is $153,000–$168,000.

Confirm GM's match, vesting rules, investment choices and payroll cutoffs. Set a percentage that captures the full match; consider an automatic annual increase only if cash flow supports it.

Keep an emergency cushion and prioritize high-interest debt. Limits are not contribution recommendations.
