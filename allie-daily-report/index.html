Refresh new-job savings settings

The 2026 employee deferral limit for 401(k), 403(b), and governmental 457 plans is $24,500; the combined traditional-and-Roth IRA limit is $7,500. For single filers, Roth IRA eligibility phases out from $153,000 to $168,000 modified AGI.

Check the employer match, emergency cash, debt rate, plan fees, and eligibility before adjusting payroll. The IRA limit is shared across traditional and Roth accounts.
