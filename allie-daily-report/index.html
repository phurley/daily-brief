Wyldwood asks narrow questions

Tabletop Titans' dice-free skirmish game uses 30-card faction decks. Its prelaunch community is invited to weigh in on art, models, cards, and rules while receiving early access and exclusive content.

Make feedback usable: choose one weekly decision for four weeks—wording, icon clarity, two art directions, or a rule variant—and publish the result.

Delay is cheaper than an unready launch

Kickstarter's February 26 Paws and Leaves case study says the relaunch reached nearly 200% after clearer responsibilities, community warming, daily content, and streamer work. It began with 630 backers against a 600-backer aim and reportedly raised €60,000 with €600 in marketing and visibility spending.

Build a readiness scoreboard: sign-ups, completed tests, would-play-again rate, three booked creator/demo contacts, and one named owner for weekly posts.
Case study ↗

Keep game one physically tiny

Kickstarter's 2026 Micro Game May guidance describes compact, complete games as easier to ship, learn, and play, and reports an 84% goal-meeting rate for those projects. The event ran May 1–31, so use this as a scope lesson—not a current deadline.

Price the lean version: start with an envelope or card-only product. List every non-card component separately and cut anything that does not create a signature decision; hold deluxe pieces until demand is proven.

Money + practical AI

Useful, not noisy

New-job retirement check-in

The 2026 employee elective-deferral limit for a 401(k), 403(b), or governmental 457 plan is $24,500; the IRA contribution limit is $7,500. Spread evenly over 26 biweekly paychecks, $24,500 is about $942 per paycheck before plan constraints.

In a partial year, capture every matching dollar first, then revisit after the first few paychecks. Confirm the formula, vesting, payroll cutoff, and combined personal deferrals across employers.
IRS bulletin ↗ · Contribution limits ↗

Put the 2027 Saver's Match on the calendar

Recent IRS guidance says eligible savers can receive up to 50% of the first $2,000 contributed to a qualifying retirement plan or non-Roth IRA—up to $1,000. For 2027, a single filer receives no match above $35,500 MAGI; phase-down starts above $20,500.

Keep a contribution record and check Form 8880-A when filing for 2027. Eligibility, destinations, and withdrawal rules are specific; the match is not for Roth IRAs.

Start with one evidence-backed AI workflow

A strong first candidate is a weekly performance-variance brief: compare approved metrics with the prior period, list the largest changes, link each claim to its source, and flag questions for analyst review. Define the recurring job, KPI and baseline, approved context and tools, evidence requirements, and a human review point.
