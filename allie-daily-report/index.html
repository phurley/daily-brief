Cyclémental keeps a debut compact

A 2–5 player, 20–40 minute card game built around one 60-creature, hand-painted Wu Xing deck and a clear win condition: collect three Qi cards.

Steal the exercise, not the theme: write a one-sentence player promise, player count/time, and visible win condition; see if a stranger can repeat all three after a five-minute teach.
