Saver's Match: a 2027 note, not 2026 cash

The match can equal up to 50% of the first $2,000 in qualifying retirement contributions, subject to income rules. For a single filer, 2027 MAGI of $35,500 or more is ineligible. Mechanics are still being developed, so do not budget around it.
