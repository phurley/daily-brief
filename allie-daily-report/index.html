Confirm the match before choosing the percentage

For 2026, the employee contribution limit is $24,500 for 401(k), 403(b) and governmental 457 plans. The combined traditional-and-Roth IRA limit is $7,500 for someone under 50; for single filers, direct Roth IRA contributions phase out from $153,000 to $168,000 of modified AGI.

During GM enrollment, verify the match formula and vesting rules, then choose a sustainable per-paycheck percentage that captures the full match without squeezing near-term cash.

The IRA cap is shared across traditional and Roth accounts, and eligibility or deductibility depends on tax situation and plan details. Educational information, not individualized tax or investment advice.
