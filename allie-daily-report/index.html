Retirement

Start with the employer match

For 2026, the employee contribution limit for 401(k), 403(b), and governmental 457 plans is $24,500; the IRA limit is $7,500. For single filers and heads of household, direct Roth IRA contributions phase out at modified AGI of $153,000–$168,000.

Confirm the plan's match, vesting, payroll deadline, and fund menu. Balance take-home pay, emergency savings, debt, and eligibility.
