Confirm the retirement match first

Health benefits

Verify HSA eligibility and remaining room

The 2026 HSA limit is $4,400 for self-only coverage and $8,750 for family coverage. Eligibility depends on coverage and other health-account coverage, not merely on an HSA being offered.

Check payroll elections, employer contributions and annual room. Coverage changes, an FSA or HRA, and employer deposits can change the allowable amount.
