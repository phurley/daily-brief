Science & technology

GeoCryoAI maps the Arctic’s zero-curtain phase

A NASA-led team combined 62.71 million in-situ measurements with 3.3 billion remote-sensing observations to map near-freezing soil conditions across the circumpolar Arctic at 30-meter resolution. The analysis reports a longer spring zero-curtain phase and regional differences relevant to permafrost carbon feedbacks. These are model-derived candidate conditions, not direct continent-wide measurements of methane or carbon release; the journal labels the article unedited pending copyediting. NASA summary

Evidence pushes the Milky Way’s merger history farther back

Analysis of Hubble data and globular-star-cluster properties finds evidence that a dwarf galaxy dubbed LKH merged with the young Milky Way about 11.8 billion years ago, extending the reconstructed assembly history by about 1.8 billion years. This is a reconstruction from present-day cluster evidence, not direct observation, and no independent replication was identified in the collector’s review. NASA announcement

Study models microbial persistence near the lunar south pole

A Science Advances study modeled several human- and spaceflight-associated microbes and found that shaded, small-scale features could permit dormant survival for at least one Earth day, raising a contamination-control concern. It did not demonstrate growth or replication on the Moon, and the authors report no evidence that lunar conditions supply what reproduction requires.
