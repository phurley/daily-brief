Nine butterflies became more than 1,300

MSU researchers and John Ball Zoo turned nine known wild Poweshiek skipperlings into a captive-and-wild population above 1,300. The inch-long butterfly survives in only a few prairie-fen populations, including two in southeast Michigan.

The recovery is real but fragile: researchers still cite habitat loss and insecticides as major threats.

A new way to watch heat move through electronics

Ultrafast X-rays now let researchers measure heat flow through several layers of a working chip at once, exposing microscopic bottlenecks that conventional surface measurements miss.

Caveat: The technique is a laboratory measurement platform, not yet a new cooling product.

Molecular electronics becomes repeatable at chip scale

A fabrication platform integrated sub-nanometer molecular layers into more than 1,000 devices that survived tens of thousands of electrical cycles, a step toward practical molecular-scale computing components.

Caveat: The result demonstrates scalable, durable test devices; commercial applications still require further development.
