New Horizons finds evidence of recent liquid on Pluto

Reanalysis of New Horizons imagery suggests liquid nitrogen may be rising through cracks at the northern edge of Pluto’s Sputnik Planitia. It is a rare clue that the dwarf planet’s surface can still move material today.

Caveat: this is an interpretation of imagery collected during the 2015 flyby, not a direct observation of liquid flowing today.
