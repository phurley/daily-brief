Nine butterflies became more than 1,300

MSU researchers and John Ball Zoo turned nine known wild Poweshiek skipperlings into a captive-and-wild population above 1,300. The inch-long butterfly survives in only a few prairie-fen populations, including two in southeast Michigan.

The recovery is real but fragile: researchers still cite habitat loss and insecticides as major threats.

Construction robots take on repetitive drilling

August Robotics says its fleet platform is moving beyond floor marking into autonomous drilling, with DALE developed alongside DEWALT for commercial construction. The goal is to make a dangerous, repetitive task more precise and less manual.

Training helped free an entangled humpback whale

A trained response team, joined by a local fishing vessel off Oregon, removed gear from a young humpback whale in July. The rescue shows how practice, specialized tools, and cooperation can make a difference even when the animal’s injuries remain severe.
